# Privacy Policy — RC Trivedi Coaching App

RC Trivedi Coaching Center ("hum") yeh app apne students ke liye chalata hai.

**Kaun si jaankari li jaati hai:** student ka naam, mobile number, pita ka naam, pata, class/batch, attendance, test ke uttar/result, aur fees payment ki jaankari (amount, mahina, UPI transaction ID). Admission form me naam, mobile aur sandesh.

**Kyon:** sirf coaching ki padhai, attendance, result aur fees ka record rakhne ke liye.

**Kahan rakhi jaati hai:** Google Firebase (Firestore) par surakshit roop se. Jaankari kisi teesre vyakti ko bechi ya di nahi jaati.

**Payment:** fees UPI app (PhonePe/Google Pay/Paytm aadi) dwara hoti hai; app aapka bank/card detail nahi leta.

**Data hatana:** apna data hatwane ke liye coaching se sampark karein.
