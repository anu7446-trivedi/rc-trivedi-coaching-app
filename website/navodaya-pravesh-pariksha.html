Navodaya Vidyalaya Class 6 Pravesh Pariksha (JNVST) — Poori Jaankari

नवोदय विद्यालय कक्षा 6 प्रवेश परीक्षा — पैटर्न, सिलेबस और तैयारी

Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya (JNV) me Class 6 me pravesh JNVST (Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test) ke dwara hota hai. Ye pariksha Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS) karwati hai. Har zile me ek Navodaya Vidyalaya hota hai, jahan padhai, rehna aur khana muft hota hai.

Exam Pattern (परीक्षा पैटर्न)

BhaagPrashnAnk
Mansik Yogyata (Reasoning / Mental Ability)2025
Paryavaran Adhyayan (EVS)2025
Ankganit (Maths)2025
Bhasha (Hindi)2025
Kul80100

Kul samay 2 ghante. Sabhi prashn objective (MCQ) hote hain; galat uttar par ank nahi katte. Naveenatam jaankari ke liye NVS ki official website (navodaya.gov.in) dekhein.

Kaun de sakta hai? (योग्यता)

  • Jo student Class 5 me padh raha ho, usi zile ke sarkari/manyata prapt school me.
  • Umar lagbhag 10 se 12 varsh (sahi janm-tithi seema NVS ki notification ke anusaar).
  • Graameen kshetra ke bacchon ke liye 75% seatein aarakshit.

Syllabus (सिलेबस) — NVS Prospectus ke anusaar

Section 1 (a): Mansik Yogyata — Reasoning (Prashn 1–20)

Ye bina shabdon wala (non-verbal) test hai — sabhi prashn chitron aur aakritiyon par aadharit. Isme 5 bhaag hain, har bhaag se 4 prashn:

  1. Pattern Completion (pattern poorti) — akshar aur sankhya pattern sahit
  2. Figure Series Completion (chitra shrinkhla poorti)
  3. Geometrical Figure Completion (jyamitiya aakriti poorti — tribhuj, varg, vritt)
  4. Mirror and Water Imaging (darpan aur jal pratibimb)
  5. Embedded Figure (chhupi hui aakriti)

Section 1 (b): Paryavaran Adhyayan — EVS (Prashn 21–40)

20 prashn: 15 MCQ + 1 gadyansh (passage) par 5 prashn. Vigyan, samajik vigyan aur paryavaran shiksha ki moolbhoot jaankari.

  • The Natural World (prakritik sansaar): parivahan, nadiyan, parvat, thal aur jal ke paudhe-jeev, prakritik aapdayein, ghar/aashray ke prakar, jal chakra aadi.
  • The Human Body (manav shareer): bhojan aur poshak tatva, swachhta, gyanendriyan, aantarik tantra ki jaankari — pachan, rakt sanchar, shwasan aadi.
  • Science in Daily Life (dainik jeevan me vigyan): bhojan sanrakshan, jal aur vayu pradooshan, jal aur mitti ka sanrakshan aadi.
  • Social Surroundings (samajik parivesh): Bharat ke sabse bade/lambe/oonche (superlatives), rajya aur rajdhaniyan, rashtriya prateek, alag-alag bhoo-drishya, tyohar, ritu, van, faslein, kapde aur reshe aadi.

Section 2: Ankganit — Arithmetic (20 prashn)

  1. Sankhya aur sankhya paddhati — aarohi/avarohi kram, nikattam 10/100/1000, sankhyaon ke naam aur maan
  2. Poorn sankhyaon par chaar moolbhoot sankriyaen (jod, ghatana, guna, bhag)
  3. Gunankhand aur gunaj tatha unke gun
  4. Bhinn aur unki sankriyaen — saman bhinnon ka jod-ghatana aur guna (asaman bhinn aur bhinnon ka bhag shamil nahi)
  5. Maap — lambai, dravyamaan, dhaarita, samay, dhan aur ikaai parivartan
  6. Sankhyatmak vyanjakon ka saralikaran
  7. Parimaap aur kshetrafal — bahubhuj ka parimaap; varg, aayat aur tribhuj (aayat ke bhaag ke roop me) ka kshetrafal
  8. Kon ke prakar aur unke saral upyog (dishayein aur nakshe sahit)
  9. Aankda vishleshan — dand aarekh (bar diagram), talika aur chitralekh (pictograph)

Zor concepts ki samajh aur unke upyog par rahega.

Section 3: Bhasha — Language Test (20 prashn)

4 gadyansh (passage) diye jaate hain, har gadyansh ke baad 5 prashn. Uddeshya — padh kar samajhne ki kshamta (reading comprehension) ki jaanch. Gadyansh dhyan se padh kar uttar dene hote hain.

Srot: NVS (Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti) JNVST Prospectus. Naveenatam jaankari ke liye navodaya.gov.in dekhein.

Taiyari kaise karein?

  1. Chaaron bhaag barabar ank ke hain (25-25) — har vishay ko roz samay dein.
  2. Roz Reasoning ke chitra wale prashn hal karein.
  3. EVS ke liye Class 3–5 ki paryavaran ki kitabein padhein, aur gadyansh wale prashno ka abhyas karein.
  4. Maths ke har chapter ke basic sawaal pakke karein.
  5. Roz kam se kam ek Hindi gadyansh padh kar 5 prashno ke uttar dene ka abhyas.
  6. Har hafte ek full model test paper 2 ghante me hal karein.
