Navodaya Vidyalaya Class 6 Pravesh Pariksha (JNVST) — Poori Jaankari

नवोदय विद्यालय कक्षा 6 प्रवेश परीक्षा — पैटर्न, सिलेबस और तैयारी

Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya (JNV) me Class 6 me pravesh JNVST (Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test) ke dwara hota hai. Ye pariksha Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS) karwati hai. Har zile me ek Navodaya Vidyalaya hota hai, jahan padhai, rehna aur khana muft hota hai.

Exam Pattern (परीक्षा पैटर्न)

BhaagPrashnAnkSamay
Mansik Yogyata (Mental Ability / Reasoning)405060 min
Ankganit (Arithmetic / Maths)202530 min
Bhasha (Language — Hindi/English)202530 min
Kul801002 ghante

Sabhi prashn objective (MCQ) hote hain; galat uttar par ank nahi katte. Naveenatam jaankari ke liye NVS ki official website dekhein.

Kaun de sakta hai? (योग्यता)

  • Jo student Class 5 me padh raha ho, usi zile ke sarkari/manyata prapt school me.
  • Umar ki seema NVS ki notification ke anusaar (aam taur par 9–13 varsh).
  • Graameen kshetra ke bacchon ke liye 75% seatein aarakshit.

Syllabus (सिलेबस)

Mansik Yogyata (Reasoning)

Odd figure out, figure matching, pattern completion, figure series, analogy, geometrical figure completion, mirror image, punched hole pattern, space visualisation, embedded figure.

Ankganit (Maths)

Sankhya aur sankhyaatmak padhati, poorn sankhya par sankriyaen, gunaj aur gunankhand (LCM/HCF), bhinn, dashamlav, maap (lambai, bhaar, samay, dhan), doori-samay-chaal, kon, pratishat, laabh-haani, saral byaaj, parimaap, kshetrafal, aayatan, data (bar graph / chart).

Bhasha (Hindi / English)

Gadyansh (passage) padh kar uttar dena — shabd gyan, vyakaran aur samajh.

Taiyari kaise karein?

  1. Roz Reasoning ke 30–40 prashn — ye sabse zyada ank wala bhaag hai.
  2. Maths ke har chapter ke basic sawaal pakke karein.
  3. Roz ek gadyansh padh kar uttar likhne ka abhyas.
  4. Har hafte ek full model test paper 2 ghante me hal karein.
  5. Galat sawaalon ki copy banakar dohrayein.
