Navodaya Vidyalaya Class 6 Pravesh Pariksha (JNVST) — Poori Jaankari

नवोदय विद्यालय कक्षा 6 प्रवेश परीक्षा — पैटर्न, सिलेबस और तैयारी

Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya (JNV) me Class 6 me pravesh JNVST (Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test) ke dwara hota hai. Ye pariksha Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS) karwati hai. Har zile me ek Navodaya Vidyalaya hota hai, jahan padhai, rehna aur khana muft hota hai.

Exam Pattern (परीक्षा पैटर्न)

BhaagPrashnAnk
Mansik Yogyata (Reasoning / Mental Ability)2025
Paryavaran Adhyayan (EVS)2025
Ankganit (Maths)2025
Bhasha (Hindi)2025
Kul80100

Kul samay 2 ghante. Sabhi prashn objective (MCQ) hote hain; galat uttar par ank nahi katte. Naveenatam jaankari ke liye NVS ki official website (navodaya.gov.in) dekhein.

Kaun de sakta hai? (योग्यता)

  • Jo student Class 5 me padh raha ho, usi zile ke sarkari/manyata prapt school me.
  • Umar lagbhag 10 se 12 varsh (sahi janm-tithi seema NVS ki notification ke anusaar).
  • Graameen kshetra ke bacchon ke liye 75% seatein aarakshit.

Syllabus (सिलेबस)

1. Mansik Yogyata (Reasoning)

Chitron par aadharit prashn — bhinn chitra (odd figure), chitra milan (figure matching), pattern poorti, chitra shrinkhla (figure series), saadrishya (analogy), jyamitiya chitra poorti, darpan pratibimb (mirror image), kagaz mod kar chhed (punched hole), aakriti ki kalpana (space visualisation), chhupi aakriti (embedded figure).

2. Paryavaran Adhyayan (EVS)

Hamara parivesh, parivar aur samaj, paudhe aur jeev-jantu, bhojan aur poshan, jal, aashray (ghar), yatra aur parivahan, hamara shareer aur swasthya, khel aur kaam, prakritik sansadhan, Bharat ka bhugol, itihas aur sanskriti ki saamanya jaankari.

3. Ankganit (Maths)

Sankhya aur sthaniya maan, jod-ghatana-guna-bhag, LCM/HCF, bhinn, dashamlav, maap (lambai, bhaar, dhaarita, samay, dhan), pratishat, laabh-haani, saral byaaj, parimaap, kshetrafal, aayatan, kon, doori-samay-chaal, aankde (bar graph / chart).

4. Bhasha (Hindi)

Gadyansh (apathit gadyansh) padh kar prashno ke uttar — shabd gyan, vilom, paryayvachi, vyakaran aur samajh.

Ye saamanya syllabus hai. Naveenatam aur poora syllabus NVS ki notification me dekhein.

Taiyari kaise karein?

  1. Chaaron bhaag barabar ank ke hain (25-25) — har vishay ko roz samay dein.
  2. Roz Reasoning ke chitra wale prashn hal karein.
  3. EVS ke liye Class 3–5 ki paryavaran ki kitabein dhyan se padhein.
  4. Maths ke har chapter ke basic sawaal pakke karein.
  5. Roz ek Hindi gadyansh padh kar uttar dene ka abhyas.
  6. Har hafte ek full model test paper 2 ghante me hal karein.
